# New App Icons and Splash Screens

## What changes

Planner gets a new identity everywhere the app shows up outside its own pages: browser tabs, bookmarks, the home screen on iPhone/iPad/Android, the desktop install dialog, and the screen shown while the installed app launches.

The old artwork (a black hardcover notebook) is replaced by a **spiral-bound dot-grid notebook on cream paper**, the same look as the app itself.

### Before / after

- **Before**
  - Browser tab shows a tiny 16px black notebook, blurry on retina screens.
  - On Android the home-screen icon is cropped at the edges, because the "maskable" icon is a copy of the normal one with no safe margin.
  - On iPhone/iPad, opening the installed app shows a **blank screen** until the page loads - there are no launch images.
  - The black notebook breaks the design rule against pure black.
- **After**
  - Crisp favicon at 16, 32 and 48px, and a sharp icon on retina screens.
  - Android shows the notebook centered on a cream circle/squircle, never cropped.
  - iPhone and iPad show a **cream launch screen with the notebook in the center**, portrait and landscape, on every current device (iPhone 6 through iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone Air, every iPad from 9.7" to 13" M4).
  - Android shows the same cream launch screen, built by Chrome from the app's name, cream background and icon.
  - The sidebar logo and login screen logo use the new notebook, sharp on retina.

### Launch screen color

Every launch screen uses the app's cream (`#f5f0e8`), not white. The icon generators returned white backgrounds, which would flash white, then cream, when the app opens. The notebook keeps the exact size and position the Progressier generator chose for each device.

### Performance

Launch images are only downloaded by the one iOS device that matches them. They are **not** added to the offline cache, so regular visitors do not download 48 extra images.

### What does NOT change

- App name, colors, theme switcher, and every page layout stay the same.
- No new dark-mode launch screen (the app has no dark theme; the "white" background option keeps using its own theme color once loaded).
- No native app-store packages (Windows Store tiles, Xcode icon sets) - only what a web app needs.

## Where the images come from

The artwork was generated by the user with four online tools. Each contributed differently:

| Tool | Used for | Not used, and why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PWABuilder Image Generator | Master transparent notebook (`android/launchericon-512x512.png`) and tightly cropped tiny sizes (`windows/Square44x44Logo.targetsize-*`) | Windows tiles and iOS app-store icons: only needed for native packaging |
| Progressier | Device list, media queries, and notebook size/position for all 48 iOS launch images | Its white background: redrawn on cream |
| PWA Icon Generator | Cross-checked the meta tags and splash sizes | Its maskable icon is a byte copy of the normal icon; only 7 devices covered |
| MagicBell | Nothing | Dark brown background baked in, `theme_color: #000000`, no maskable icon |
